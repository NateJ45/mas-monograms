MAS Monograms respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you submit the quote form, we collect the details you provide — your name, email address, phone number, the project details you describe, and any reference photos you choose to upload. To keep the form free of spam, we use Cloudflare Turnstile, which may process limited technical data (such as your IP address) to tell humans from bots.

  • We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.
  • We only ask for what we need to prepare your quote and complete your order.

How we use your information

We use your information solely to respond to your quote request, prepare your order, and communicate with you about it. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

How your information is handled

Quote submissions are delivered to us by email through Resend, and a copy is stored securely so nothing is lost in transit. These service providers process your information only to provide those services to us.

Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly, cookie-free website analytics to understand which pages are visited. This does not identify you personally.

Your choices

You can ask us to access or delete the information you have shared at any time — just email us at mastone37@gmail.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at mastone37@gmail.com.
