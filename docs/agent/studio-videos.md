# Studio tutorial videos: the scripts

Written 2026-10-19 (studio-complete, Phase A). Recorded in Phase B with the kit's tutorial-video pipeline (starter
card 137: Playwright against the local Studio with sample data, captions burned in, mp4 and webm, every caption frame
checked). Each finished clip goes in `public/guides/<guide id>.mp4` and the guide's `video` field
(`src/sanity/guides/`), which puts it in "Watch how it is done" on Home and in the Site Guide.

Rules for every clip: 1280 wide for the desk, 390 wide for the phone ones; under two minutes; light appearance; the
account avatar and any name covered; **sample data only** (the photos, names and dates below are made up and are not
customers); captions in her plain voice, no em-dashes, one line each, on screen at least 2.5 seconds; nothing is
actually published (the pipeline runs against a local Studio with the sample data, never the live dataset).

Sample data used across clips:

- Photos: three square sample pictures from `docs/references/` or generated swatches, described as "Navy monogram on a
  white hand towel", "Pink name on a baby blanket", "Gold initials on a canvas tote".
- Clearance item: "Sample tote, linen" with a sale price of 20 and one left.
- Price tag: "Sample tag" at 10.
- Quote requests (Messages): "Sam Sample" asking about napkins, "Alex Example" asking about a hat. Never a real
  person's name, email or phone.
- Dates: a Christmas deadline of December 1, an away week of July 6 to July 12.
